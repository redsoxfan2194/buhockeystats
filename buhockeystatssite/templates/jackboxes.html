Jack's Boxes

Jack the Stats Pup has created a grid of some of his favorite stats, but he's having trouble deciding which Terrier to put in each box. He needs YOUR help to fill out the grid in this daily game.

How to Play Jack's Boxes

Click a box to search for a player, then click Select to submit your answer.

Select a player for each box that matches the criteria for that box's row and column

You have nine (9) shots at filling out the grid

Each shot, right or wrong counts as a shot

A player cannot be used more than once and once a box is filled it cannot be changed

Click Give Up to see your score

Click a number under Possible Answers to see the answers for that box

You can play each Jack's Boxes game once per day
